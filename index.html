Sofia and Zürich. One app.

Sofia combines official timetables with live surface-vehicle positions, departure updates and service alerts. Metro positions are estimated from the timetable.

Zürich uses Swiss timetable data, official ZVV line colours and matching route geometry where available. Vehicle positions are timetable estimates, not live GPS. Some services, including trains, still use straight connections between stops.

Choose your city in Settings. Zürich requires version 2.1.5 or later.
